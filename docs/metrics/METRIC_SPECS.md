A metric is named by `id@version`. A change to a definition bumps the version; a version is never reused for a different definition. Below its minimum sample a metric is shown with its sample size and no headline.

## Bid fill rate (`fill-rate@1`, measured)

Of all bids placed in finalized crosses that printed, the share that received any fill. A bid that was cancelled or never revealed counts as placed and not filled.

- Formula: `bids with fill > 0 / bids placed`
- Unit: share, 0 to 1
- One observation is a bid; headline from 30 observations
- Computed by `executionQuality` in `services/api/src/metrics.ts`, served at `/v1/metrics/execution` (`bids.fillRate`)
- Spec hash: `7fc8fc35531e36cd`

Caveats:
- Devnet traffic is mostly the operator's own test wallets; see external-flow-share before reading this as user experience.
- A bid priced far from the market is expected to go unfilled: the rate is not a quality score by itself.

## Price improvement of a filled bid (`price-improvement@1`, measured)

For a filled bid, its own limit price minus the single clearing price it paid, as basis points of the limit. Reported as the median and the 10th percentile (nearest rank) over filled bids.

- Formula: `(limit - clearingPrice) / limit x 10000`
- Unit: basis points
- One observation is a filled bid; headline from 30 observations
- Computed by `executionQuality` in `services/api/src/metrics.ts`, served at `/v1/metrics/execution` (`priceImprovement.bps`)
- Spec hash: `048aa7c5ee8321c6`

Caveats:
- A bid placed exactly at the clearing price improves by zero by construction.

## Share of bid escrow returned (`refund-share@1`, measured)

Per finalized cross, the share of bid escrow returned to bidders (the refund on claim plus escrow returned on cancel), summarised as median, minimum and maximum across crosses.

- Formula: `escrow returned / escrow committed, per cross`
- Unit: share, 0 to 1
- One observation is a cross; headline from 10 observations
- Computed by `executionQuality` in `services/api/src/metrics.ts`, served at `/v1/metrics/execution` (`refundShare`)
- Spec hash: `f5203144468332ce`

Caveats:
- High is not good or bad in itself: it measures how much of what bidders put in was not needed.

## Time from close to finalized (`close-to-finalized-seconds@1`, measured)

Seconds from a cross's close timestamp to the block time of its CrossFinalized event. Median, 90th percentile and maximum; crosses with an unreadable block time are excluded and counted.

- Formula: `blockTime(CrossFinalized) - closeTs`
- Unit: seconds
- One observation is a cross; headline from 10 observations
- Computed by `executionQuality` in `services/api/src/metrics.ts`, served at `/v1/metrics/execution` (`closeToFinalizedSeconds`)
- Spec hash: `402e83a5d83e6586`

Caveats:
- Depends on a keeper being online: a stopped keeper shows up here as a long tail.

## Largest-buyer concentration (`top-buyer-concentration@1`, measured)

The proportion of finalized crosses in which the largest single buying wallet took more than a threshold share (default 10%) of the buy-side fill, with a Wilson 95% interval taken over crosses.

- Formula: `crosses where top-1 buy share > threshold / crosses`
- Unit: proportion with 95% interval
- One observation is a cross; headline from 20 observations
- Computed by `fairness` in `services/api/src/metrics.ts`, served at `/v1/metrics/fairness` (`topBuyerAboveThreshold`)
- Spec hash: `c5998a03bbfb6145`

Caveats:
- Wallets are not people: one actor using many wallets looks more dispersed than it is. integrity funding clusters address part of this.

## Share of flow that is not ours (`external-flow-share@1`, measured)

Wallets that are not in OWN_WALLETS and are not a cross's creator, proceeds recipient, integrator or fee recipient, as a share of all wallets with orders.

- Formula: `external wallets / wallets`
- Unit: share, 0 to 1
- One observation is a wallet; headline from 30 observations
- Computed by `externalFlow` in `services/api/src/metrics.ts`, served at `/v1/metrics/external-flow` (`wallets.externalShare`)
- Spec hash: `472c6330af260b0d`

Caveats:
- An upper bound on outside users: any wallet the operator runs that is not listed counts as external.

## Share of crosses that printed (`print-rate@1`, measured)

Crosses that cleared or finalized with a clearing price, over all crosses indexed.

- Formula: `crosses printed / crosses total`
- Unit: share, 0 to 1
- One observation is a cross; headline from 20 observations
- Computed by `buildServer` in `services/api/src/server.ts`, served at `/v1/stats` (`crosses_printed / crosses_total`)
- Spec hash: `80830df1fbcd2626`

Caveats:
- A cross that did not print because nothing crossed is working as designed, not failing.

## Coverage of signed clearing-price bands (`promise-coverage@1`, measured)

Of signed promises of a clearing-price band that were scored, the share whose realised price fell inside the band, per confidence bucket, with a Wilson interval and the nominal coverage beside it.

- Formula: `hits / (hits + misses)`
- Unit: proportion with 95% interval
- One observation is a scored promise; headline from 30 observations
- Computed by `report` in `packages/promises/src/accuracy.ts`, served at `/v1/accuracy` (`buckets[].coverage`)
- Spec hash: `e1a28e25266bd7cb`

Caveats:
- A band is CALIBRATED only after the gate in ADR-0015 is met; until then it is PROVISIONAL and says why.

## Organic volume, lower and upper bound (`organic-volume-bounds@1`, experimental)

Lower: share of volume from bidders shown independent (a traced funder shared with no other bidder, no tie to the issuer). Upper: share of volume not shown inorganic (minus conflicted volume and cross-side funding-cluster self-trade). The truth lies between.

- Formula: `independent volume / total; (total - conflicted - self-trade) / total`
- Unit: basis points of volume, two bounds
- One observation is a order, weighted by base atoms; headline from 30 observations
- Computed by `organicVolume` in `packages/integrity/src/organic.ts`, served at `/v1/integrity/cross/:address/organic` (`lowerBps, upperBps`)
- Spec hash: `431b56e3e194d388`

Caveats:
- Not validated against labelled real wash trading. No Jito bundle data.
- First-funder tracing only.

## Volume from conflicted bidders (`conflicted-volume-share@1`, experimental)

Share of a cross's known volume carried by bidders tied to the issuer by evidence on chain (the issuer, its payee, a wallet it funded, or one that shares its funder).

- Formula: `volume of flagged orders / volume of orders with known volume`
- Unit: basis points of volume
- One observation is a order, weighted by base atoms; headline from 1 observations
- Computed by `findConflicts` in `packages/integrity/src/conflicts.ts`, served at `/v1/integrity/cross/:address/conflicts` (`flagged.volumeBps`)
- Spec hash: `aef9c3589e1ef6c2`

Caveats:
- A wallet that is not flagged is not proven independent.

## Protocol fee revenue, verified (`protocol-fee-revenue@1`, measured)

Protocol and integrator fees of finalized crosses whose three sources agree to the atom: the cross counters run through the program's fee rules, the CrossFinalized event, and the finalize transaction's token balance changes. Lines that could not be verified are counted separately and never summed.

- Formula: `sum of protocolFee over MATCH lines, per quote mint`
- Unit: quote atoms per mint
- One observation is a finalized cross; headline from 1 observations
- Computed by `ledgerEntry` in `packages/fees/src/ledger.ts`, served at `/v1/fees/ledger` (`totals.verified`)
- Spec hash: `5412460e05decb27`

Caveats:
- Devnet fees are of no monetary value. Revenue to the company is the share of these that reaches the fee wallet, which is a deployment fact.
