14 of 15 equal to the atom. The one that differs (-300 of 42 million, 0.7 bp) is the first sell after a buy on the ladder pair: the quote read the chain at one second, the simulation ran later, and the pair's variable fee decays with time (filter period 10 s). The time probe in the same run (sell 5,000,000 base right after a buy, six reads 4 s apart) shows it: the first read quoted 6,542,315 inside the filter window and the simulation 18 s later gave 6,542,370; the next five quotes were 6,542,370 and equalled the simulation exactly. That reading of the difference is supported by the probe, not proved by replaying the pre-swap state.

The minimum is enforced by the program: a DAMM swap built with a minimum one atom above the quote was refused by the cluster (`ExceededSlippage`, custom error 6002), and the API refuses the same request earlier (409 `MINIMUM_ABOVE_QUOTE`).

The API refused, with reasons, what it could not do: buying beyond a pair's remaining liquidity (409 `POOL_CANNOT_ABSORB`, with the amount it could take).

## X8 against a real cross (`scripts/cross-depth-proof.ts`)

A launch cross on the deployed cross_core `3RmtYyznG4MxWs1hhsiQrEx2nKHC4zeoFAyyunksTWU7` with an open book (issuer offers 1,500 at 1.00 and 1,500 at 1.30; bids 1,000 at 1.60 and 1,800 at 1.30).

- The route's baseline (1.30, 2,800 matched) equalled the verifier's reference clearing of the same four orders.
- The route predicted a market buy of 400 tokens would leave the price at 1.30 (matched 3,000); after that order was placed the route read 1.30.
- It predicted a market buy of 2,500 tokens would move the price to 1.60 (+2,308 bps); after that order was placed on chain the route read 1.60.
- The cross settled on chain at 1.60 with 3,000 matched (tx `36rFhkKH…`), the price the route had predicted.

## Screens (real Chromium, real wallet signing, devnet)

- Look run (`scripts/pools-web-e2e.mjs`, `MODE=look`): 8 pages (finder, three pool pages, issuer view, depth of a finished and of a live cross, dashboard), light and dark, 1440 and 390 wide. 56 of 60 checks pass, including no horizontal scroll and axe WCAG 2.2 AA on every page checked. The 4 failures are the same check, zero console and HTTP errors, each caused by `HTTP 429` from the public devnet RPC to the browser's own token-metadata reads: an external rate limit, not a defect in the pages.
- Trade run (`MODE=trade`): in the browser, the connected wallet priced a buy of 5 tokens on the ladder pair (page: pay 6.555495, receive about 5, at least 4.975), signed it and it landed: 5,000,000 base atoms arrived and exactly 6,555,495 quote atoms left, equal to the page's figure.
- Screenshots were looked at and read critically (layout, tables, chart). One fix came out of it: the impact tables overflowed their column and now stack.

## NOT covered

- The sell leg of the trade run through the browser did not complete: the public devnet RPC rate-limited the dev API so hard (every route retried on 429) that the sell quote never returned in time. The sell path was proved through the API route (above), not through the browser.
- The cross/Jupiter legs of `/v1/pools/route` were not seen on a live server: the route merges into the answer of `/v1/router/quote`, which needs the indexer database; its merge is tested against a stub of that answer. Jupiter prices mainnet only.
- Mobile layout was checked for overflow and axe, and screenshots taken; only the desktop ones were examined closely.
- Not exercised on chain: limit-order liquidity, DLMM collect-fee mode 1, slot-activated pools, DAMM fee-scheduler and rate-limiter pools, token-2022 transfer-fee mints (constructed state only), mints with transfer hooks (refused), native SOL (refused), pools with bin arrays beyond the nine read.
- Pools other than the ones the adapters open for a pair cannot be listed (no scan of the programs); `pool=` names one.
- pump.fun and MetaDAO comparison, and per-venue volume (row X8): not built; no verified source for their curves and no swap events in the index.
- Persistence is observed by the API process since it first read the pool and is not stored.
- `/v1/router/quote` itself still excludes Meteora; the pool-priced route is `/v1/pools/route`.
