# W2D-B14: hard-to-game ending (B14, B11, VM3)

Outcome: **BUILT-LOCAL**, with two limits stated below. Proved on LiteSVM; devnet proof pending funding. Design: ADR-0452.

What is on chain: an ending period cut into 2 to 8 buckets, each bucket in its own shard; a cancel cutoff; a drawn last counted bucket (candle auction) with a two-step permissionless draw from four spaced `SlotHashes` samples; one drawn extension when a cross fails its guards; explicit tie-breakers (already, ADR-0003).

Tests (`tests/core/test/rules-ending.test.ts`, 8 tests, all passing): orders land in the bucket of the instant they were placed; cancels close before the ending period and stay closed (decoy orders cannot be withdrawn); the clear needs the draw and a draw cannot finish early; the drawn end decides which buckets merge, the price, the volume, and each order's balance on chain (later buckets are refunded in full); a bucket after the end cannot be merged (`BucketExcluded`); a draw whose block hashes left the sysvar is void and restarts; a cross that fails its guards gets one drawn extension with a length inside the published range and its orders cannot be cancelled; a second failure is final; an extension nobody draws clears after the three-day grace period. Rust unit tests: placement is monotone and never past the last bucket; auxiliary shard bits are distinct; `required_aux` follows the drawn end. `rules-compute.test.ts` runs a sealed cross with the ending period and a random end through reveal, merge, clear, claim and finalize.

Limits, said plainly: (1) the randomness is the cluster's attested block hashes, not a verifiable random function; there is no VRF on devnet and none is used (the draw state separates begin, seed and apply so a VRF seed can replace `draw_finish`). NOT VERIFIED against a real cluster's slot hashes: `scripts/deploy-batch-2.sh` runs that proof. (2) "Indicative price delayed and imbalance shown only in coarse buckets" and "a 20% move forces buyers to re-sign" are not enforceable on an open book: the coarse view is `coarseIndicative` (`@opening-cross/cross-core-client`) for the API and apps to apply (not wired by this work), and the re-sign rule is implemented as the price-move guard that refuses to print and refunds (ADR-0451).
