# W3D-R4-X14: multi-asset pairing, and the Meteora volume engine (decision: no program)

Outcome: **NOT DOING in the programs; services and router.** ADR-0659. Rows R4, X14, R1.

- R4: every settlement adapter already takes the quote mint from the cross, and ADR-0485 proved a stock-like quote mint on DLMM (`W2D-X12.md`); which pairs exist is a fact of the market. Cross-pair routing is `packages/router`.
- X14, R1: reopens (`Y1`), DLMM and Alpha Vault pools opened at P*, the batch window and the greenshoe (`W2D-X13.md`) exist. "Routed to Meteora by default" is a console and router default, not an on-chain rule (a forced venue would also conflict with the graduation vote, ADR-0656). The headline metric is a read of public accounts.

`| X14, R1 Meteora volume engine | PARTIAL | Reopens, DLMM and Alpha Vault pools at P*, batch windows and the greenshoe are built (`W2D-X13.md`, `W2D-D4`); no program is needed (ADR-0659) | An indexer table and an API route for the fees and TVL of pools opened through the adapters (derived from the cross, public accounts), the console default, and the headline figure on the landing page, labelled with its sample |`

## What the app layers must add

Indexer: for each cross, the addresses its adapters create (the DAMM v2 pool and position, the DLMM pair, the Raydium pool, the MetaDAO DAO) are derived from the cross; project their reserves and the fees the pools report, per day. API: `GET /v1/metrics/meteora` with the sample size and the date range. Router: quote across two pools with different quotes through a common asset. Console: the default venue for a new cross, with the graduation vote option. Not covered here: any of it.
