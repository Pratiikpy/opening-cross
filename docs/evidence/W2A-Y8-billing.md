# W2A-Y8: issuer subscriptions (`services/billing`)

Date: 2026-10-13. Code: `services/billing` (chain, store, routes, JSON-RPC chain, `main.ts`), migration `013_billing.sql`; tests: 13 (against Postgres 18), `tsc` clean. Decision: ADR-0304. Prices: the proposed rate card (`packages/fees`, `proposed` until HD1 is decided).

## What it does

`GET /v1/billing/plans` lists the plans and says the prices are proposals. `POST /v1/billing/subscribe { plan, issuer }` returns one unsigned transaction the issuer alone signs (and pays the fee for): create the revenue wallet's token account if it is missing, transfer the plan price, and a memo `oc-sub:1:<plan>`. `POST /v1/billing/payments { signature }` reads the transaction back from the chain: the amount is the revenue token account's own balance change (not any figure in the instruction text), the payer is the authority of the transfer into that account, the plan is the memo; a failed transaction, a transfer of another token, a transaction with no memo and a payment with no balance change are each refused with a reason. A payment is recorded once by signature. `entitlementPeriods` (from `packages/fees`) turns payments into the periods they buy: a payment below the plan price buys nothing and is listed as rejected, and paying early extends the running period. `GET /v1/billing/entitlements/:issuer` returns the plan in force and the recurring crosses the issuer created in that period against those included. `POST /v1/billing/invoices` returns an invoice signed by the billing key (price, overage beyond the included crosses, paid, due); an open period's invoice is a preview and is not kept, a finished period's is dated its end, so issuing it twice yields the same signed document, kept once. Entitlement is a fact other services (the scheduler, the keeper) read; it never gates the program.

## Tests

The payment parser against hand-built transactions (every refusal; the amount taken from the balance, with a lie planted in the instruction); the built transaction decoded: one signer who is the fee payer, three instructions, token and memo programs present, no signature yet; a mint that does not exist refused; the routes over Postgres: plans, subscribe (and a bad plan or issuer), a payment recorded once and a repeat reported not fresh, an early second payment extending the period, a below-price payment and a payment for a plan that does not exist buying nothing and being listed, an unknown signature 404 and a non-payment 422, recurring crosses counted in the period (five counted of six, one of another kind ignored) and the overage invoiced (124 USDC due = 99 plan + 25 for one cross over four), the signed invoice verifying, open versus finished periods, no invoice without a signing key, no invoice where nothing was paid.

## A real subscription on devnet

`scripts/devnet-run.ts`: a throwaway issuer wallet (funded 0.02 SOL by the ops key) bought the starter plan with 120 test USDC the ops key minted on the mm-run quote mint. The service built the payment; the issuer signed and sent it (transaction `4NbfyB9SEsLfGQLw4jibojxNhgVRW3G2aTDAL4uvRmi9DGhPj5F6YKv2tkF3bxpbmgKEzP52X17sKF6DG9ciArbR`, slot 510705724); reported to the service, it read back 99.00 USDC from issuer `3JqaMApy...` for `recurring-starter`, recorded it (201), and a second report was recognised (200, not fresh, one period). The entitlement showed one 30-day period starting at the block time; the invoice verified against the billing key `DTB3Jtxb...`. Independent of the service, the revenue wallet's token account on devnet held exactly 99,000,000 atoms.

**A defect the run found:** the machine's clock was one second behind the chain's block time, so the answer to the issuer who had just paid was "not entitled": the period starts at the payment's block time, which was in the future of the answer's "now". The default time is now never earlier than the issuer's latest payment (a test pins it); a request that asks for an explicit earlier time still gets "not entitled".

## Not covered

The prices are proposals (HD1); the rate card is not published. No subscription lapse handling beyond periods ending (no renewal reminders, no dunning). No refunds or cancellation. USDC on mainnet was not used; the mint is configurable and the run used a test mint. The scheduler and keeper do not yet read entitlements. The x402 gate is not used for plan payments: a plan is a 30-day entitlement tied to a payer wallet, which a direct transfer proves more simply; x402 serves the per-call products. A real payment from a wallet extension was not driven.
