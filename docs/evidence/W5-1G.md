## Defects found and fixed (each was reproduced before the fix)

1. **The keeper could not finalize a cross that names a referrer whose token account does not exist.** `finalize` refuses with `ReferralAccountMissing` (6065); `buildFinalizeWithAccounts` opens the other three payees' accounts and not the referrer's. Reproduced on the real binary (control below). Fixed in the keeper (ADR-0963); the SDK builder (outside this package) still lacks it.
2. **A scoped keeper never served what its series spawned.** `spawn_next` makes the series' own address the creator; `KEEPER_CREATORS` (required by compose) matched creators only. Fixed in discovery and in the acceptance test every duty uses (ADR-0963).
3. **Billing usage never counted a series' crosses**, for the same reason; invoices could not show the overage the scheduler creates. Fixed (ADR-0960).
4. **The fee ledger would have called every referral cross a `MISMATCH`** (it expected the fee recipient to receive the whole protocol fee). Fixed (ADR-0962).
5. **A blank `KEEPER_DUTIES` meant no duties at all**, and `KEEPER_DRY_RUN` was not passed by compose. Fixed; `default` is now accepted inside a list (ADR-0964).
6. The status text "the keeper builds the rules tail and drives drawn endings, but not `push_refunds`, lock release or challenge expiry" was stale: the keeper has sent all three since ADR-0703. Shown below.

## Y8: issuer subscriptions

Built: the keeper's `series` duty reads the issuer's entitlement from the billing route (`KEEPER_BILLING_URL`) and spawns only for subscribers; exempt list `KEEPER_ENTITLEMENT_EXEMPT`; stale-on-error with an entitled answer never outliving its period; nothing a user's money depends on is conditioned on it. Billing counts the crosses of the issuer's series. ADR-0960.

Proved: `entitlements.test.ts` (16): the HTTP client against a real HTTP server, the series duty on the in-memory chain, and the statement that clearing, claims, finalizing and lock releases are done for an unsubscribed issuer. `entitlements-consumer.test.ts` (8, real Postgres): the keeper's client against the real billing routes after a payment read back from a chain double: entitled with plan and period end, below-price payment buys nothing, block time ahead of the clock is entitled at once, an early second payment extends the period, the period ends, 4 of 8 inserted crosses counted by series and period.

Not covered: the gate against a real chain (a series spawned by the keeper on a validator only if entitled); the billing service and keeper running as separate processes under compose; real USDC (the plan is paid in the configured mint, a test mint in every run); prices (proposals, HD1), dunning, refunds, cancellation (ADR-0304).

## W4 / W6: the ledger records the referrer's share

## The real-validator proof with non-zero fees (rows W4, W6, V-E, X6/J1/P6 "non-zero fees untested")

`services/keeper/scripts/referral-proof.ts`, run 2026-10-16 on the Agave 4.0.2 validator above, `RPC_URL=http://127.0.0.1:8950 pnpm --filter @opening-cross/keeper exec tsx scripts/referral-proof.ts`. Protocol schedule 30 / 5 / 15 / 30 bps, initialised by the run. The keeper's own `runOnce` (real RPC, a keeper wallet that is not the creator) did every lifecycle step. The ledger is `packages/fees` reading the finalize transaction's token balances and the `CrossFinalized` event decoded from the transaction's logs by the indexer's decoder.

| Cross | Fees | Result |
|---|---|---|
| A: mint mode, integrator 200 bps, referral 10%, **the referrer has no token account** | protocol 30 bps: event 7,200,000; integrator 48,000,000; referral 720,000 | MATCH; the referrer holds 720,000; its account was created by the keeper in the finalize transaction `mCXtKK6N...` |
| B: mint mode, integrator 120 bps, no referral | protocol 7,200,000; integrator 28,800,000 | MATCH `23eqBpnz...` |
| C: escrow mode, integrator 80 bps (the protocol fee is capped at a quarter of it: 20 bps), referral 50%, referrer has an account | protocol 4,800,000; integrator 19,200,000; referral 2,400,000 | MATCH; the referrer holds 2,400,000 `5K5WCFjT...` |
| D: mint mode, a minimum raise nobody meets (rules written with a zero-share referral record) | no print, fees 0 | MATCH with zero fees `2keY94oR...`; the keeper sent **one `push_refunds` for all three orders** (checked as the transaction's only cross_core instruction, by discriminator), the three buyers hold exactly what they held before, `ordersOpen` 0, state Finalized |

Control on cross A, same state, before the keeper finalized it: the SDK's `buildFinalizeWithAccounts` simulated on the validator fails with `AnchorError ... ReferralAccountMissing. Error Number: 6065. The referrer's quote token account is missing or does not match.` The keeper's transaction, which adds the account creation, succeeded. Keeper actions sent in the run: settle 4, claim 9, finalize 4, push-refunds 1; no failures other than the four finalize attempts the script held back on purpose. The fee recipient's balance rose by exactly (7,200,000 - 720,000) + 7,200,000 + (4,800,000 - 2,400,000) in the run, as the ledger says.

What this does not show: lock release and challenge expiry sent by the keeper on a real chain. Expiry waits 14 days on chain time; a locked fill needs a class-2 order under an identity gate. Both are shown sent by instruction discriminator on the in-memory chain (`lifecycle-sends.test.ts`, 6) and their builders are exercised against the real program by `tests/core` (`rules-refunds.test.ts`, `sdk-rules-builders.test.ts`), which could not be run here: NOT VERIFIED in this session.

## P2: the keeper claims the protocol's partner fees

Built: duty `partner-fees` (ADR-0961), opt-in, scheduled, minimums, reads the pool by IDL offsets, opens the fee recipient's two token accounts, one transaction.

Proved: `partner-fees.test.ts` (19): the layout against Meteora's IDL (offsets 264 and 272, size 8 + 416, both discriminators) and Anchor's decoder reading the keeper's bytes; the planner at its boundaries; the pass on the in-memory chain (the destinations are the cross's fee recipient, the pool is emptied, a pass inside the interval does not read the pool, more accrued is claimed after it, a below-minimum pool waits, no pool / live cross / unbound mint / junk bytes are quiet, a refused claim fails only its cross and is retried at the next interval, scope, dry run, off unless named). On a real validator, `services/keeper/scripts/partner-fee-proof.ts` (2026-10-16, Agave 4.0.2 with the repository's `cross_core`, `cross_gate`, `cross_settle`, the DBC fixture and the metadata program): a deferred-mint launch cleared, `open_dbc_pool` at P*, bind, two claims, finalize, a trade of 50 quote tokens on the live pool (a `TransferHookPool`, 1% flat fee, quote collection, no creator share).

| Step | Result |
|---|---|
| Pool after the trade, decoded by Anchor from the IDL | partner quote fee 400,000; protocol quote fee 100,000; creator 0; partner base fee 0 |
| The keeper's reader | the same two numbers |
| Keeper pass 1 (`partner-fees` only, interval 5 s) | one transaction `claim-partner-fee`; the fee recipient of the cross (a wallet the keeper does not control) went from 1,875,000 to 2,275,000: **received 400,000**; the pool's partner counters are 0; protocol and creator shares unchanged |
| Pass 2 inside the interval; pass 3 after it with an empty pool | nothing sent, nothing failed |
| A second trade of 20 quote tokens, pass 4 after the interval | **received 160,000**, equal to the pool's accrued partner fee |

A first run of this script printed `received 0`. The script, not the keeper, was wrong: the validator already held a protocol account from the earlier run, so the cross's fee recipient was that run's wallet, not the script's. The keeper had paid the cross's recipient, which is the rule. The script now reads the recipient from the cross, and the run above is on a fresh validator.

Not covered: devnet and mainnet (the `cross_settle` upgrade that carries `claim_partner_fee` is not deployed, H27); the DBC fixture's equality with mainnet's program; a pool with a base-token fee (the layout test covers the offset, the real run had none); the partner's migration fee, surplus and pool-creation fee, and the creator's share (ADR-0961); the transfer-hook gate refusing the base leg when the fee recipient has no pass (it would be reported as a failed claim and retried at the next interval).

Verified: `deploy/docker-compose.yml` passes `OWN_WALLETS: ${OWN_WALLETS:-}` to the API, `.env.example` documents it, `services/api/src/main.ts` reads it with `parseOwnWallets`, the API's parser accepts a comma or whitespace list and refuses a value that is not an address, and with the keeper's and the market maker's keys listed, `externalFlow` counts their orders as own (4 wallets: 2 own, 2 external; 3 filled wallets: 2 own, 1 external) while unset it counts all four as external and says the figure is an upper bound. ## X6 / J1 / P6: MPP and x402 over the API's own routes, and non-zero fees

`packages/x402/test/api-paid-routes.test.ts` (12; 11 without `MPP_INTEROP_DIR`): `registerPaid` from `services/api` behind the real `X402Gate`, a real facilitator, the real Postgres ledger and the 1.18.26 validator. Unpaid calls show both challenges (x402 requirements and `WWW-Authenticate: Payment`) with a real sample, and move nothing. `verify`, `bundle` and `order-desk` (a POST) are each bought over MPP with the package's client: the response is the signed document (verifies against the published key), the payer's balance falls and the recipient's rises by exactly the rate-card price (10,000, 50,000, 20,000), the receipt's transaction has no error on chain, and the ledger row says `mpp` with the payer. The same over x402. The official `@solana/mpp` client in a separate process buys `verify` holding tokens and no SOL (the server sponsors the fee). The careless buyer: bad address 400, unknown cross 404, unsettled cross 409, empty or off-grid or malformed order 400, each with no challenge and no price and nothing charged; a client whose limit is below the price signs nothing; a failure after the payment verified (the RPC behind the order desk is down) charges nothing and writes no ledger row; a credential paid for one order buys nothing for a changed order (`invalid-challenge`, "the payment was not made for this request"). The suites that were already there (`payments.test.ts`, 54 on both token programs including the official-client test) were skipped before and now pass.

Non-zero fees on a real chain: the proof above. The market-maker devnet cross had a zero fee (`W2A-W4-fees.md`); these four had fees from 30 bps down to the capped 20 bps, in mint and escrow mode, with and without a referral.

Not covered: the deployed API (pay-per-call is off until a payment mint and recipient are set, H29), mainnet, devnet, the public facilitator settling a payment (its `/supported` and `/verify` shapes are exercised by an existing test that needs only the network), a wallet extension paying, a Blink client unfurl, a mainnet Jupiter path.
