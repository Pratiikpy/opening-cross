# W2D-W11: halt-resume triggers (W11)

Outcome: **BUILT-LOCAL**. Design: ADR-0455.

Triggers: the issuer's pause (`sync_pause` copies a Token-2022 pausable mint), the issuer authority or a venue signer (`set_halt`, `set_corporate_action`), the oracle circuit breaker (`observe_price`: a verified Pyth update; a move above `move_bps` between two observations within `window_slots` halts; updates older than 120 s or not newer than the last are refused). A halt binds crosses that name the halt state; nothing but a printing resume cross, created by the halt state's authority or venue signer (a stranger's wash cross cannot lift a halt: refused at `set_rules`), lifts it, and the print is recorded and feeds lenders.

Tests (`rules-safety.test.ts`, `rules-guards.test.ts`, `rules-stocks.test.ts`): the breaker trips inside the window and only then; stale, replayed, partly verified and wrong-feed updates are refused; a paused mint is copied and a cross refuses to print and refunds, and pays out once the issuer resumes; a stranger cannot create a resume cross; a halted reopen does not print, the authority's resume cross prints, lifts the halt and feeds lenders, the next reopen prints. Two defects of the first draft found by these tests and fixed: anyone could create a resume cross, and a stale price could be replayed into the breaker.

Not covered: Chainlink or other oracles (Pyth only); a real Pyth equity feed (H11); the venue signer's identity (HUMAN_TASKS HS2).
