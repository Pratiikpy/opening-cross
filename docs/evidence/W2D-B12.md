# W2D-B12: anti-flip lockups (B12)

Outcome: **BUILT-LOCAL**, partial. Design: ADR-0454.

Built: optional buyer lockups (class 2 with a cliff and linear vesting, set by the buyer above the cross's minimum), mandatory insider lockups (declared insiders' fills locked behind a cliff and vesting), identity-bound vesting for class 1. Locked fills are paid into a per-cross lock vault (minted straight there in mint mode, so no transfer hook runs) with a `Lockup` record funded from the order's own rent; `release_locked` pays the vested part to the owner, callable by anyone. A refund never needs lock accounts. Lock rules are refused on deferred-mint crosses (a hooked release needs forwarded accounts; not verified).

Tests: `rules-classes.test.ts` (class-2 fills vest on a clock; nothing before the cliff, half at the midpoint, all at the end, record closes), `rules-insiders.test.ts` (insider cliff), `rules-safety.test.ts` (a locked claim without its accounts is refused; a refund needs none), `rules-stocks.test.ts` (an unlock released and sold in a reopen), Rust property tests (vested never exceeds the amount and never decreases).

Not built: "lower future priority for fast flippers" (a reputation figure kept off chain).
