# W5-1E: insider, team and treasury alerts (F5) and signed, anchored receipts (S2)

Date: 2026-10-16. Cluster: devnet. ADRs: 0940 (alerts), 0941 (receipts). Screenshots: `docs/evidence/screens/w5-1e/` (quantized, 1.1 MB).

## F5: alerts on insider, team and treasury movements

### What was built

`holder-alerts-core.ts` (topics `insider`, `team`, `treasury`, movement facts, wording, ordering), `holder-alerts-store.ts` (scopes, baselines, recorded movements, treasury outflows from `treasury_movements`), the new `holder-alerts-watch.ts` (who is tied to a token, the balance scan, RPC client), `holder-alerts.ts` (availability per topic, `GET /v1/alerts/subject`, feed look-back, scan scheduling in the delivery loop), and on the web `components/alerts/movement-alerts.ts`, `movement-section.tsx`, and edits to `alerts-page.tsx` and `use-alerts.ts`.

### Proved

Unit and route tests (`services/api/test/holder-alerts-movements.test.ts`, 26 tests, real Postgres via PGlite with the indexer's own migrations 006, 012 and 020; Solana replaced by a recorder): topic gating (`team` is included in `insider`, never the reverse), scope (a cross covers its token and that token's treasury), nothing sent from before a subscription or older than the look-back, ordering and a cap of 20 per pass, wording with no "sold"/"dump"/"rug", baselines (first scan alerts nothing, small falls add up, rises raise the baseline, a day-old baseline is replaced, an unreadable wallet keeps its baseline), a signed team list that is tampered or for another token is ignored, treasury outflows exclude inflows, holder redemptions, undated rows and other treasuries and name a team or authority counterparty, a database without the treasury tables answers with nothing, topics unavailable on a deployment are refused with the reason (422) and reported in `/v1/alerts/config`, push delivery of an insider and a treasury alert exactly once over three passes, the feed starting a scan for the token it was asked about. Existing `holder-alerts.test.ts` (27) still passes. `apps/web/test/holder-movement-alerts.test.ts`: 6 tests (storage parsing, the 50-address limit shared with crosses and markets, merging into the server's lists, wording).

Real devnet, no browser (`services/api/test/alerts-movements-devnet.ts`): the cross `2VpFruAR…K29H` (created by our devnet wallet `DTB3…KEdC`) sells Token-2022 mint `7HHSa4g3…D8Aa`, supply 3,960,000,000, the creator holding 100%. The scan found one insider with its reason (`created cross 2VpF…K29H, which sells the token`), took a baseline, a real transfer moved 118,800,000 atoms (3%) out (`5nMsdCgtvApio9vMkug7XdE1G6BkEbfETpeNc71YrueG95T1eDXN97urWX3MMStxx8h6tUsiz3jZip6DzwkEEkUj`), and the next scan recorded `beforePpm 1000000, afterPpm 970000, movedPpm 30000` with that same signature as the wallet's newest transaction. Item: "Insider wallet DTB3…KEdC moved 3.00% of token 7HHS…D8Aa out". A second identical run (`2fyRe847…`) behaved the same; both returned the tokens. The same script reads the 8 real events of live devnet treasury `3SeQ…avsw`, which the indexer's `recordMovement` turns into 5 value-moving rows; the alert layer reads 3 outflows (the 2 redemptions are not alerts), each worded as "spent from its allowance … team outflow … recorded in transaction …".

Real browser (`apps/web/e2e/w5-1e-alerts-movements.mjs`, production build, Chrome, 30 of 30 checks, final run): the three topics are enabled; looking up the cross lists the token and the creator with the reason; Follow keeps the choice across a reload; a treasury is recognised; an address that is nothing says so with no Follow button; junk is refused with a reason; a real 3% transfer (`4YDWsiHarj4x…c2w`, returned by `3EcB7Nnph58g…ShzBcpN`) appears in the feed as the insider alert with its reason and limit, "just now", linking to the cross; zero console errors; no horizontal overflow at 1440 and 390, light and dark; axe finds no serious or critical violation.

### Not covered (state it, do not imply it)

Email and Telegram delivery of a movement alert against a real provider (the path is ADR-0400's, the item is an ordinary `AlertItem`); a push to a real browser for a movement. The team topic against a real issuer's signed declaration on devnet (tested with generated signatures only). The treasury topic live in a browser: the real events are four days old, outside the 24 h feed window, so the feed is empty for them; the path is proved at the database and wording level on real events, and in a browser only as "available". A token with a declared insider in a cross's rules or a treasury extension (the readers are exercised by the code path but no devnet cross with declared insiders was used). Mainnet tokens. Firefox and Safari.

Incident, owned: two earlier runs of the browser script killed the transfer script before it returned the tokens, stranding 2 x 118,800,000 atoms (6% of that supply) in two fresh wallets whose keys were not kept. They are test tokens of our own finalized devnet cross; the creator holds 94% instead of 100%. The script now waits for the return and the run above returned its tokens.

## S2: shareable fairness receipt, signed and anchored

### What was built

`services/promiser/src/receipt-attestation.ts` (the signed document, memo text, canonical JSON), `receipt-store.ts` (tables created on first use), `receipt-round.ts` (attest and anchor rounds), `receipt-view.ts` (the API answer), `main.ts` (rounds wired, `RECEIPT_URL`/`WEB_URL`); `packages/verifier/schema/receipt-attestation.v1.schema.json` and `receipt-attestation-view.v1.schema.json` with fixtures captured from the run below; on the web `app/receipt/_attestation/*` (verification, loading, panel), `/receipt/<cross>/attestation`, and the panel under both receipt pages.

### Proved

`services/api/test/receipt-attestation.test.ts`, 10 tests, PGlite with the indexer schema, cross accounts encoded by the program's encoder, receipt bytes from the web app's own `buildReceipt`: a settled cross is signed once its receipt agrees with the cross account, and the document verifies with `@opening-cross/attest` `verifyDocument` against the published key; altered, mis-signed and foreign documents are refused; a no-print cross is attested and a receipt claiming no print for a cross whose account has a clearing is not; 15 refusals each with its own reason (not settled, unsupported proof, unreachable, malformed, not canonical, header mismatch, wrong cross, wrong network, not final, recomputation did not match, chain disagrees, not on chain, wallet receipt); crosses that cannot be attested do not crowd out others and are retried later; a receipt whose bytes change gets a second attestation and the first is kept; anchors batch under their own memo prefix with a proof that reaches the root and fails for any other id; a tampered anchor is refused; the route answers 400, 404, soft 200 and the view with the published-signer verdict. `apps/web/test/receipt-attestation-verify.test.ts`, 10 tests: the web verifier accepts what the promiser signs (same id, same signature, Merkle proofs up to 13 members) and fails on each way of altering it; no check is passed that was not made. `packages/verifier/schema/schema.test.ts`: 15 new of 37.

Real devnet (`services/api/test/receipt-harness.ts`, with a fresh signer `2KyN6qVvWWzr1jmKrks158B9P5bFni9zqiExtBSQPvBY` funded 0.05 SOL): the promiser rounds fetched the real receipts of the live index's settled crosses from the production web server, read each cross account over RPC, and signed 100 of 101 (the 101st got a 503 from the web app's API read, was backed off and left unattested; no signature was forced). No cross was skipped for disagreeing with its account, so the receipt's result fields and the cross account agree on all 100. One Memo transaction anchored all 100: `5WWavLP6ZGhQpgomK2ngTXeLC5j9aj9HMqCVozhdfB4o84oiHFySdr9Xcnh6E3GfSw3KujiSiDWKn6DoEWiF3erm`, slot 511462428, block time 1792094316, memo `opening-cross/receipts/v1 root=90cc85b1…93b7 n=100`.

Real browser against a Next production build (`apps/web/e2e/w5-1e-receipt-attestation.mjs`, 48 of 48 checks): for cross `2gS3…1ReZ` the panel reads "Signed by Opening Cross and anchored on chain" with all six checks passing, the anchor read from the chain by the web server (`id`, `signature`, `signer`, `receipt`, `merkle`, `chain`), at 1440 and 390 px, light and dark, no overflow, zero console errors, axe clean at serious/critical. A settled cross with no attestation says "Not signed yet" and shows no check; a live cross has nothing signed; a wallet receipt says it carries no signature and links to the cross receipt; after one field of a stored attestation was altered the page showed "Do not rely on this attestation" with `id` and `receipt` failed and the JSON route reported the same. The JSON receipt hashes to its `x-receipt-sha256` header and to the attested hash.

The image route in a production build (the gap in the status table): `/receipt/<cross>/image` answered 200, `image/png`, 1200 by 630, `public, max-age=3600`; the wallet image the same; an unsettled cross got the "Not settled yet" notice image with `no-store`; a junk address 404. Built with `NEXT_DIST_DIR=node_modules/.cache/next-1e` and deleted afterwards.

### Not covered

A cross that settled without a print, on devnet: the live index lists none, so that state is proved only with encoded cross accounts (test) and not seen in a browser or as an image. `/v1/crosses/:address/receipt-attestation` is not registered in `services/api/src/server.ts` (outside this package): the harness registers the same three lines. The promiser process itself (`main.ts`) was not run with Postgres; its rounds were run by the harness with the same code and a real Memo transaction. A second promiser racing the first. Mainnet. Wallet receipts are deliberately not signed (they change when an order is claimed).
