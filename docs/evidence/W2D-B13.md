# W2D-B13: float and FDV guardrails (B13)

Outcome: **BUILT-LOCAL**. Design: ADR-0451.

Minimum public float (`min_float_bps` of the declared or on-chain supply) and maximum FDV-to-raise ratio (`max_fdv_x100`) are checked at the clear on the **counted** volume (declared insiders excluded). Met exactly prints, one basis point short does not (`rules-guards.test.ts`); the guard arithmetic is a pure function in `clearing-core` with unit tests and 400+ shared vectors; `rules-compute.test.ts` runs them with every other rule on.

Limits: a deferred-mint cross has no mint to read, so it must declare the supply; the declared supply is the issuer's word, bounded by the mint authority being revoked at finalize.
