# W3D-D10: graduation by holder vote (`cross_graduate`, governed seeding in `cross_settle`), proved on LiteSVM

Status 2026-10-14: **program, rule, gate, client and tests built; proved on LiteSVM against the real DAMM v2 binary and the real `cross_core`; not deployed (H27; the gate needs the batch 2 upgrade of `cross_core` and `cross_settle`).** Not audited. ADR-0656. Rows D10, Z0.

## What was built

- `cross_core`: one rule flag, `FLAG_GOVERNED_GRADUATION` (`1 << 5`), accepted by `set_rules`. A `u16` flag in the existing rules tail: no layout change, no migration (1,191,504 to 1,191,512 bytes).
- `cross_settle`: `graduation.rs`. Every seeding instruction (`seed_damm_v2`, `seed_damm_v2_batched`, `open_dlmm_pair`, `open_dlmm_pair_batched`, `open_dlmm_pair_badged`, `seed_raydium_cpmm`, and the new `seed_futarchy_dao`) reads that flag from the cross. An ungoverned cross takes no extra account and no extra check. A governed cross needs the `Outcome` PDA of `cross_graduate`, as the last remaining account, owned by that program, for this cross and this venue; otherwise `GraduationNotApproved` (code 6029) stops the call before any CPI.
- `cross_graduate` (new program `47f6BhDPCxmW9C9DuuMS2VnbCoU4wnSvjReEwy9Zdke7`, 329,576 bytes, 7 instructions, 7 events, 19 errors) and the client `@opening-cross/cross-graduate-client`: `init_charter` (creator, before the first order, on a governed cross: quorum 10% to 100% of the live supply, a window of 3 to 30 days, an optional minimum organic volume and its attester), `start_round` (creator, after the cross is finalized; three rounds at most, one at a time), `cast_vote` (locks tokens behind DAMM v2, DLMM, Raydium CPMM or MetaDAO until the window ends; add to a stake, never change venue), `post_volume` (the attester), `finalize_round` (anyone: quorum, strict majority of what was locked, volume), `publish_outcome`, `withdraw_vote`.

## Tests

- `tests/core/test/graduate.test.ts`: **8 passed**, against the real cp-amm binary. Without a passed vote the creator's `seed_damm_v2` is refused (no outcome account, and an outcome account that does not exist); after a passed vote for DAMM v2 the pool opens at P* and the voter gets the stake back; an outcome that names DLMM is refused by the DAMM adapter, an outcome copied to another address is refused, an outcome at the right address and with the right bytes but owned by another program is refused and the same bytes owned by `cross_graduate` are accepted, the Raydium adapter refuses the DLMM outcome, the DLMM adapter without an outcome is refused and with it gets past the gate. The vote: a charter needs sane terms, the creator, a governed cross and no orders yet; a round starts only for the creator after the cross is finalized and one at a time; tokens are locked until the window closes, cannot change venue, cannot vote after, and come back after; a round under quorum fails, a 50/50 split fails, 1,001 against 1,000 passes; three failed rounds use all attempts; the volume gate fails with no figure or one atom short and passes at the minimum, and only the attester can post it.
- `cargo test -p cross_graduate`: 5 passed (a strict majority of a quorum wins, ties and a missing quorum do not; the volume gate; no overflow; the flag offset, cross body size and discriminators pinned against the real `cross_core` types). `cargo test -p cross_settle`: 17 passed (3 are new: the layout `graduation.rs` reads is `cross_core`'s and `cross_graduate`'s, `governed()` is read from the rules flags, and the futarchy checks).
- Mutation checks (the change applied, the program rebuilt, the suite run, the change reverted): quorum check removed -> 1 test fails; majority `<=` to `<` -> 1; volume gate removed -> 1; withdraw before the window closes -> 1; venue lock removed -> 1; charter's governed check removed -> 1; creator-only round start removed -> 1; the gate off in `cross_settle` -> 2; the venue comparison removed -> 1; the outcome owner check removed -> 1 (this survived until the owner-forgery test was added). **One survives by design:** replacing the outcome's PDA comparison with an always-true one passes every test, because an account owned by `cross_graduate` with the `Outcome` discriminator and this cross's key can only be the PDA (the program creates outcomes nowhere else). The comparison is kept as defence in depth.
- `cargo fmt --check` and `cargo clippy -D warnings` over the workspace: clean.

## Size and deploy cost

`cross_graduate` 329,576 bytes: program account rent 1.675 SOL (permanent) and a returned buffer (the batch 3 estimate prints the total). `cross_core` and `cross_settle` grow inside batch 2 (see `W3D-deploy.md`).

## Not covered

Devnet; a DBC-launched market (its migration is permissionless and a vote cannot gate it, ADR-0486); holders' quorum against an issuer that still holds a mint authority (a veto by dilution, stated in ADR-0656); organic volume itself (attested, not measured); the full vote-to-seeding path for DLMM, Raydium and MetaDAO (the gate for each is tested; the vote that chooses them is the same code); an audit.

## What the app layers must add

SDK: `FLAG_GOVERNED_GRADUATION` in `rules-check.ts` (its settable mask lists four flags and refuses the fifth), builders for the seven instructions, and the outcome as the last remaining account on the seeding builders of a governed cross (`packages/adapters`: `buildSeedDammV2`, `buildOpenDlmmPair`, `buildSeedRaydiumCpmm`). Indexer: project the 7 events and the `Charter`, `Round`, `VoteRecord`, `Outcome` accounts. API: charter, round and tally reads; unsigned-transaction routes. Issuer console: the rule in the rules step, the charter step (quorum, window, volume gate), a banner on a cross whose token still has a mint authority; vote page for holders; keeper: `finalize_round` and `publish_outcome` when a window closes. Diagnose: add the 19 errors and `GraduationNotApproved`.
