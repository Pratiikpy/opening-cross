# W2D-B9: allocation policies (B9, T3)

Outcome: **BUILT-LOCAL**, partial. Design: ADR-0453.

Built: reserved shares of the rationed marginal volume for small verified buyers (class 1), buyers who accept a lock (class 2, lock-weighted) and priority buyers (class 3: early commitment, or a holder or supporter pass), with spill-over so a reserve a class cannot fill goes to everyone; early commitment improves priority and never price. Better-priced orders always fill in full; only the marginal tick is split. Sum of fills never exceeds the volume (property test and vectors).

Not built: bottom-up fill, merit or holder reservation from an on-chain balance snapshot (a supporter credential is a gate pass instead), reputation-based priority for flippers (an off-chain figure).

Tests: `rules-classes.test.ts` (class and lottery cross with fills compared to the reference), `rules-random.test.ts` (24 random books), `rules-verifier.test.ts`, clearing-core unit tests, `packages/verifier/conformance/v2.json` regenerated byte-identically from the program's own code.
