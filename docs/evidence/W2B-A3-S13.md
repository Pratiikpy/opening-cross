# A3, S13 pre-raise demand check and demand map (2026-10-13)

Outcome: BUILT for the shareable draft, soft commits, the demand map by price and by wallet track record, and the sizing tool. NEEDS-PROGRAM for the refundable-deposit waitlist. Demand by region is NOT built on purpose.

## What exists

- `/issuer/demand` (publish a draft listing, signed by the wallet), `/issuer/draft/[id]` (public page labelled "Not yet verified", the demand map, the sizing against the offer, the commit form), `/issuer/simulator?draft=<id>` and the simulator's "use real interest from a draft" (the soft commits become demand).
- API (ADR-0353): `draft` and `softcommit` records; a commit's price must lie on the draft's grid and buy at least one atom; one commit per wallet per draft (a later one replaces it); the demand map computes tokens exactly (budget x 10^decimals / price, floor).
- Wallet track record: for each committer, whether it bid or was filled in an earlier cross on this network, from the indexer's `orders` table.
- Sizing (`lib/issuer-demand.ts`): demand at or above each price against the offer, and the highest price at which the whole offer is still covered.

## Checks

`services/api/test/issuer-board.test.ts` (21): the grid and amount refusals; 120 quote at 1.20 is 100 tokens, 150 at 1.50 is 100 and 90 at 1.50 is 60; the per-level and per-track-record totals; replacement of an earlier commit. `apps/web/test/issuer-demand.test.ts` (6) and `issuer-growth.test.ts`. Browser (`e2e/issuer-console.mjs`): a stranger sees "Not yet verified" and what connecting does; the holder's commit of 300 quote at a limit of 1.50 became exactly 200 tokens in the API and on the page; the wallet with no earlier cross was grouped as "no history"; 200 of 300 offered tokens is stated as not covering the offer; a second commit replaced the first (400 tokens); the simulator loaded the draft's grid, offer and commits and cleared them at 1.5 for 300. Screens: `console-05-draft.png`, `console-06-simulator.png`.

## Not built

- Refundable-deposit waitlist: a deposit needs escrow. A cross is the only escrow the programs have, so a deposit waitlist means a program change (a refundable deposit account per draft) or a real cross.
- Demand by region: not collected. The service holds no location data, and the page states that it will not infer one.
- Identity or sybil resistance beyond one commit per wallet: a wallet is not a person.
- A shareable draft stored only in this deployment's database: nothing publishes it elsewhere.
