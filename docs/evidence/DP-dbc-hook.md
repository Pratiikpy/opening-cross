## Findings

1. **The DBC SDK cannot resolve owner-dependent hook accounts.** `getRemainingAccountsForTransferHook` resolves extra accounts against `PublicKey.default` source and destination, so any hook whose extra accounts are seeded from token-account data (ours: pass and lock PDAs keyed by owner) throws `TokenTransferHookInvalidSeed`. The DBC *program* accepts correctly resolved accounts; this proof resolves them against the real vault and wallet. Our adapter must do the same, and the fix is worth upstreaming to Meteora's SDK.
2. **DBC removes the transfer hook when the curve completes** (`process_swap.rs` `revoke_transfer_hook`; confirmed above: hook program becomes the null address). Passes and locks therefore stop being enforced at graduation, even though lock records remain. Identity gating and lockups that must outlive the curve need Token ACL (DP8) or escrow-held vesting; a hook lock alone is not a post-graduation guarantee and must not be presented as one.
3. **Mint authority is usable before trading and revocable.** Minting 5M tokens to a winner left DBC's base reserve, vault and price unchanged, and a fixed-supply config still migrated and released its leftover. This validates the continuation design (clear the cross, mint Q* to winners at P*, revoke, then let the curve run).
