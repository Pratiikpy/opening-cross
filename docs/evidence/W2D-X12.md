# W2D-X12: stock-quoted DLMM pair with a Token Badge, proved on LiteSVM

Status: **proved on LiteSVM against the real DLMM binary; HUMAN for the real badge (H9, HS6).** ADR-0485. Test: `tests/core/test/settle-stock.test.ts` (2).

A quote mint with the extensions every listed stock carries (pausable, permanent delegate): without a badge the real DLMM refuses the pair with `UnsupportedMintExtension`; with a `TokenBadge` account for the mint, `open_dlmm_pair_badged` opens it at P*. The badge account is planted in the test because only Meteora can issue one; wrong badge-account counts are refused (`BadgeAccounts`) and a wrong badge is refused by DLMM.

Not covered: DAMM v2 and DBC with a stock quote mint; scaled-UI-amount and transfer-hook extensions on the quote mint; a real badge on devnet or mainnet.
